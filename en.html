Exclusive Apartment in Porto Cristo

Discover this stylish and fully renovated apartment in the heart of Porto Cristo, one of the most popular coastal towns on the east coast of Mallorca.

Its central location is one of its key advantages: the beach is just a one-minute walk away. The harbour, restaurants, shops and the local health centre are also all within easy walking distance.

The property offers approximately 69 m² of built area and stands out for its well-designed layout, exceptional brightness and comprehensive renovation.

The apartment comprises two bedrooms, one bathroom, a separate storage room and a spacious open-plan living area with a comfortable lounge, dining area and open-plan American-style kitchen. One bedroom features a comfortable double bed, while the second bedroom is equipped with a bunk bed.

The heart of the apartment is the generous living area, which originally consisted of two separate rooms. As part of the renovation, these rooms were combined to create an open and harmonious living concept. Today, the living area, dining space and open-plan kitchen flow seamlessly into one another.

A special feature of the apartment is the traditional patio de luz. This central, light-filled interior courtyard connects all the rooms – the living area with kitchen, both bedrooms and the bathroom. The patio allows additional natural light to enter the apartment and creates a pleasant, open atmosphere. The washing machine is also located here.

As part of the comprehensive renovation, additional wall and ceiling structures with sound and moisture insulation were installed. These measures contribute to a pleasant indoor climate and enhance living comfort throughout the year.

Continuous laminate flooring has been installed throughout the living areas, giving the apartment a modern and harmonious overall appearance. Large windows also provide plenty of natural light and create a bright and welcoming atmosphere.

A separate storage room provides practical additional storage space and is ideal for provisions, household items or personal belongings.
